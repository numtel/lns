My Path

I've been shown the most perfect people in my life. Everyone I meet is specially tailored to me it seems. We're all one and the same as the saying goes. But its more than that. My life is a story that grows. Every day, I wake up and coincidences preclude me to new situations which become obligatory. I follow the line and it keeps giving. Every day, my routine becomes stronger. I see now for what things are, working together to become better. It's all platitudes but what isn't? Here's the thing, the universe allows anything, all the time. So you better believe and think your way to a future where people are caring and loving because that's how you get there. It's as simple as that. Listen to your heart and go with your gut. We're all in this together. Love, Sen
